# Model gallery

Research models built with MARIO. Find the study, its modelling assumptions, and the available code and data.

## Steel transition

Steelmaking technologies, electricity supply and climate-policy scenarios.

* [EU steel decarbonisation](models/steel-transition-eu.md) — 2024. Compare low-carbon steelmaking routes and their environmental and market implications in Europe.
* [GCAM–MARIO steel scenarios](models/gcam-mario-steel.md) — 2026. Link GCAM technology pathways with hybrid input-output footprints for European steel policy.

## Photovoltaic supply chains

PV manufacturing, regional sourcing and the effects of reshoring.

* [Solar PV reshoring](models/pv-hlca.md) — 2026. Assess the environmental and socio-economic effects of moving photovoltaic supply chains to Europe.

## Sufficiency scenarios

Household demand changes and their contribution to European decarbonisation.

* [Sufficiency in a decarbonising Europe](models/fulfill.md) — 2025. Assess household sufficiency measures alongside changes in the European energy system.
* [Sufficiency and EU climate targets](models/fulfill-targets.md) — 2026. Examine the contribution of sufficiency to the EU’s 2030 and 2050 greenhouse-gas targets.

## Electricity footprints

Electricity emission factors across technologies, countries and data sources.

* [Electricity carbon intensity](models/electricity-carbon-footprint.md) — 2026. Compare electricity emission factors and explain differences between data sources and accounting methods.

## Shared databases

Steel, photovoltaic and sufficiency studies share roots in EXIOBASE Hybrid, with different extensions and historical editions. NXSUT provides a possible common background for future development.

[Follow the database lineage](databases.md)
