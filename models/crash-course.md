## What you can learn

- Follow the three notebooks in order: foundations, EXIOBASE, and sectors and scenarios.

**Setup documented.** The repository provides a uv environment and setup guide. Its README reports that Foundations was executed; this catalog has not independently rerun the course.

MARIO distribution: `mariopy`; version: **1.0.2**.
