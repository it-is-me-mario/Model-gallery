# EU steel decarbonisation

Lorenzo Rinaldi, Debora Ghezzi, Emanuela Colombo, Matteo Vincenzo Rocco (2024). [Assessing environmental and market implications of steel decarbonisation strategies: a hybrid input-output model for the European union](https://doi.org/10.1088/1748-9326/ad5bf1). *Environmental Research Letters*.

Compare low-carbon steelmaking routes and their environmental and market implications in Europe.

## Model and scope

Adds steelmaking and hydrogen technologies to a hybrid supply-use database and evaluates technology-penetration scenarios for the EU.

European steel production; carbon footprints and prices under alternative steel and electricity mixes.

## Database

EXIOBASE Hybrid, with EU aggregation, electricity updating and dedicated steel and hydrogen inventories. This is the earlier EU study in the steel modelling line.
